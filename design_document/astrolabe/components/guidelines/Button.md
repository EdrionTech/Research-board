# Button

Button is a small planet in pill form: solid buttons have a lit body (bright edge, deep core, shadowed side) and grow an orbit ring around them on hover; secondary buttons are the same dark sphere as the OrbitButton.

- Provide a short label (`children`, sentence case: "Launch", "Plan a night"), optional `icon`, `variant`, `tone`, `size` and `orbit`.
- `variant`: `solid` for the main action (one per view), `outline` for secondary actions (dark sphere, ink label, tone-coloured icon), `ghost` for quiet links (tone text, underline on hover).
- `tone`: `star` primary, `sun` warm secondary, `aurora` confirm, `nova` abort or delete, `nebula` discovery.
- `orbit` adds a tilted ring with a golden moon to the single hero action on a screen.
- Every label sits on a measured body colour: 4.5:1 or better in both themes. Minimum height is 44px.
