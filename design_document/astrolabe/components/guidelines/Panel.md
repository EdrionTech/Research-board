# Panel

Panel is a softly lit, slightly see-through card with hairline borders, crosshair marks at its corners and a ruler of tick marks under the header — a page from an observatory log.

- Provide `children`, optional `title` (a display-serif name), `coords` (a mono line such as "RA 05h 35m · Dec −05° 23′"), `halo` (faint orbit rings in the top-right corner) and `footer`.
- Panels sit `space-5` apart in a `.as-grid`; keep one `halo` panel per screen, for the featured object.
