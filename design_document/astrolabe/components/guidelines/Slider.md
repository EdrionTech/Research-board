# Slider

Slider is a lit orbit track over a ruler of ticks, with a planet for a knob and the value in a mono chip.

- Provide `label`, `value`/`defaultValue`, `min`, `max`, `format` (how values read, "21:00", "40×"), `tone` (fill colour) and `onChange`.
- The ends of the range are printed under the ruler; screen readers hear the formatted value.
