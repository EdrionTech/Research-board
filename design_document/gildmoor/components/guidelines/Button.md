# Button

Button is a gem-filled plate with a double gold frame and diamond end caps.

- Provide a short verb as `children` ("Continue", "Play", "Restart"), optional `gem`, `size` and `icon`.
- Gems are jobs: `sapphire` continue / primary, `emerald` play / accept, `ruby` restart / danger, `amethyst` magic, `none` (enamel) for quiet actions.
- One `lg` button per screen (the title-screen choice); `sm` in item cards and dialogs.
