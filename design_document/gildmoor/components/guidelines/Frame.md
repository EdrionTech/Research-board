# Frame

Frame is the gilded panel: a gold border with an inner hairline, diamond corners and a diamond finial at the top centre, over a deep enamel fill.

- Provide `children`, optional `title` (a `heading` in caps), `footer` (lore text), `shape` (`panel`, `arch` with a rounded top, `banner` with a pointed foot) and `gem` (tint the fill sapphire, emerald, ruby or amethyst).
- Arched frames suit portraits, quests and character sheets; banners suit vertical menus and stats.
- Frames sit `space-6` apart; don't nest frames — use a Divider inside.
