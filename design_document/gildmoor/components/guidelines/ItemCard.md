# ItemCard

ItemCard shows one item, spell or character: a gem-tinted gilded card with a ribbon title, glowing art, rarity and lore.

- Provide `title`, `icon` or `image`, `gem` (the card's tint), optional `rarity`, `children` (lore, one or two lines) and `footer` (a small button or price).
- Lay cards out in a `.gm-grid` or a row; keep every card in a row the same size.
