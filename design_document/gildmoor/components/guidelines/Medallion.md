# Medallion

Medallion is a round enamel button with a double gold ring and four diamond studs — for skill-tree nodes, inventory slots, close and settings.

- Provide `icon` and `label` (read aloud), optional `gem` (default midnight) and `size` (`sm` 44, `md` 56, `lg` 76, `xl` 104). Pass `children` to put an image inside instead of an icon.
