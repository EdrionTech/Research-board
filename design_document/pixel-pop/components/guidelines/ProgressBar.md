# ProgressBar

ProgressBar fills in whole segments inside an outlined well — never a smooth bar.

- Provide `value` (0–100), optional `label` (shown with the percentage in the `system` face), `segments` (default 12) and `tone` (`blue` loading, `green` health or success, `pink` affection or bonus, `orange` heat or danger).
- Use it for loading and for game meters (health, XP).
