# Hazard

Hazard is a warning banner edged with yellow-and-black stripes. `caution` is hazard yellow with black text; `alert` is red with white text and a blinking icon (still for people who prefer reduced motion).

- Provide `title`, `children` (one sentence), optional `tone`, `icon`, `action` (a Button) and `live` (announces it; alerts are assertive).
