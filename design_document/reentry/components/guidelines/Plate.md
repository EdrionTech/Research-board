# Plate

Plate is a machined metal panel: a lit top edge, a hard cel outline, a screw in each corner and a stencilled title over a grooved rule.

- Provide `children`, optional `title` (Russo One, uppercase), `code` (a short id in a hazard-yellow tag: "UNIT-04", "SEC 7G"), `stripe` (a hazard band along the top, for plates that hold dangerous controls) and `footer` (mono).
- Plates sit `space-5` apart in a `.re-grid`. Keep one `stripe` plate per screen.
