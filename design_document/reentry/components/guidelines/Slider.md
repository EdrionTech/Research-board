# Slider

Slider is a throttle fader: a ridged steel grip with a coloured centre line in a recessed slot, a fill in the tone colour, and a ruler with printed values underneath.

- Provide `label`, `value`/`defaultValue`, `min`, `max`, `marks` (how many printed values, 5), `format` ("72%", "3.5 G"), `tone` and `onChange`. Screen readers hear the formatted value.
