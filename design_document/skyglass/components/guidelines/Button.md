# Button

Button is a glossy pill with a white shine across its top half.

- Provide a short label (`children`, sentence case: "Accept", "Follow") and optional `icon`.
- `tone` is the job: `blue` primary, `green` accept / go, `red` decline / delete, `orange` download or attention, `aqua` secondary positive, `glass` quiet.
- One `blue` or `green` per view; `glass` for everything else.
- `size`: `sm` in bars and dialogs, `md` default, `lg` for the single hero action.
