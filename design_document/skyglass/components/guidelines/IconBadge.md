# IconBadge

IconBadge is a small glossy candy square holding one glyph — the way icons stand alone in Skyglass (sidebars, menus, settings lists, notifications).

- Provide `name`, `tone` (default `aqua`; `glass` for neutral items), `size` (`sm` 28, `md` 36, `lg` 52) and `label` when it carries meaning on its own.
- Give each section of a menu its own tone, and keep the same icon in the same tone everywhere.
