Have your projects on your other devices too: they sync through a Research Board server, yours or your group's. You always work on the copy here, online or not; changes cross within seconds whenever the server can be reached.

        Add a server

        Get an invite from the server: its window shows one, its control page makes more, and so does any device already on it (Your servers › Invite a device). Scan it, or paste its link. A server whose address changed needs only the new address.

        Have its key instead of an invite?

        Point the camera at the invite's QR code: in the server's window, on its control page, or on another of your devices.

        Synced projects

        Each project's own settings (Settings on its card, or Project on its page) put it on a server, sync it, or stop. Stopping keeps both copies.
