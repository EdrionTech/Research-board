# Research Board 0.2.0

Your projects on all your devices, kept in step through a server you run.

## New

- **Sync with a server.** Put a project on a Research Board server and it's on your other devices too, the phone app, the desktop app or a browser. You always work on the copy on the device, online or not; changes cross within seconds whenever the server can be reached.
- **Choose where a project lives.** When making one (Keep it: on this device only, or on a server), or later from its settings: put it on a server, sync now, stop syncing (both copies stay), or make a separate copy that isn't synced.
- **Edits that clash.** When two devices change the same thing while apart, the newer change is kept and the other is kept aside: the project shows it, and you choose which to use.
- **Your own server in one double-click.** `serve.bat` (or `./serve.sh`) starts it and opens its control page: its addresses, a QR code to add a device, the devices (each with its own key, removable on its own), invites for one device or several, its look, and its settings. Reachable from your network, from anywhere for testing (a temporary Cloudflare address), or from a rented server with HTTPS (`apps/server/vps-setup.sh`).
- **Adding a device:** in the app, Settings › Sync › Scan QR code, or paste the invite link.
- **Settings in tabs:** Appearance, Sync, Your data.
- **On phones:** every popup is a sheet along the bottom with its buttons always in view; the settings button shows its icon on every page.

## The files

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `ResearchBoard-0.2.0-setup.exe` (or `.msi`) | Windows: the desktop app |
| `ResearchBoard-0.2.0.apk` | Android: the phone app (allow installing apps from this source). Signed with Research Board's release key: later versions install over it, keeping your data |
| `ResearchBoard-0.2.0-host.zip` | The server: unzip, then `serve.bat` (Windows) or `./serve.sh` (macOS, Linux) |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Check a download: `certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256` (Windows), `shasum -a 256 <file>` (macOS, Linux) |

## Good to know

- The phone app installed from an earlier test build (`…-debug.apk`) has another signature: remove it before installing this one (its own data goes with it; projects on a server come back once you connect again).
- On your own network the server is plain `http`; the phone app allows that. Over the internet use HTTPS: the Cloudflare address, or a VPS set up with `vps-setup.sh`.
- One server key gives full access to that server: invite only your own devices or people you trust. Accounts, sign-ups and sharing projects between people come later.
- Not yet tried on real hardware: `vps-setup.sh` on a rented server, `serve.sh` on macOS/Linux, and "Start with the computer".
